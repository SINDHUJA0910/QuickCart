Everything on one platform

Built for both sides of the till

One system for the shopper's phone and the retailer's back office — inventory, payments, and surveillance stay in sync automatically.

📷

AI theft detection

YOLOv8 and DeepSORT watch every connected camera and flag when what's picked up doesn't match what's scanned.

Barcode self-checkout

The back camera opens automatically. No extra taps between "pick up" and "in cart."

Encrypted exit pass

A time-limited QR code — not a session ID — so a photographed pass can't be replayed at the door.

Live retailer dashboard

Real-time inventory

🧾

Digital invoices

A GST-itemized PDF generates automatically at checkout — searchable, downloadable, never lost in a bag.
